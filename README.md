# Fresh Leads | Desktop & Web Lead Generator

**Fresh Leads** is a turnkey, desktop application for finding and extracting local B2B leads, law offices, consultants, and business contact information with verified email addresses.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Fresh Leads

### macOS Users
1. Double-click **`Fresh Leads.command`**
   *(Or run `./start.sh` in Terminal)*
2. The app will automatically check dependencies, set up Playwright Chromium drivers, and open the desktop interface window.

### Windows Users
1. Double-click **`start.bat`** (or `Launch Fresh Leads.vbs`).
2. The app will launch the server and open your browser automatically at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.

---

## ⚡ Features

- **Multi-Country Support**: United States (US), Canada (CA), United Kingdom (UK), Australia (AU), Netherlands (NL), Germany (DE), Belgium (BE).
- **Target Categories**: B2B Consultants, Law Offices & Attorneys, HoReCa (Restaurants & Hotels), Real Estate Agencies, IT & Software, Healthcare & Clinics.
- **Live Terminal Console**: Real-time streaming log monitor embedded right in the UI.
- **Email Finder Engine**: Crawls company websites, contact forms, and legal pages (*Impressum*, *Kontakt*) to find verified email addresses.
- **One-Click CSV Export**: Download your compiled lead databases directly from the Lead Collections Library in the app.

---

## 📋 System Requirements
- Python 3.9+ installed on your computer ([Download Python](https://www.python.org/downloads/))
